Who we are

About

About Us

Abhinash Giri & Co. is a PCS firm based in Kolkata, advising businesses, promoters, investors and management teams on corporate law, governance, regulatory compliance and capital markets matters. Our firm supports clients across the entire corporate lifecycle, including business incorporation, structuring, governance, compliance, private funding, mergers, acquisitions, takeovers, public offerings and listing-related matters, ongoing corporate and securities law compliance, strategic transactions and other corporate restructuring processes.

Our firm was established to provide practical and commercially focused advisory, assisting clients in navigating an increasingly complex legal and regulatory environment. The practice is shaped by the founder's professional experience across company secretarial practice and capital markets assignments, enabling clients to benefit from both regulatory expertise and an understanding of transaction execution, stakeholder expectations and corporate decision-making.

Our Values

Our firm places emphasis on integrity, precision, confidentiality, responsiveness, and long-term professional relationships. Each engagement is approached with attention to statutory detail, commercial context, and the governance standards expected in professional practice. Our firm has obtained its Peer Review Certificate from the Institute of Company Secretaries of India in respect of its attestation services, valid till August 31, 2028.

Professional Background

Our firm is led by CS Abhinash Giri, a Practicing Company Secretary based in Kolkata, whose professional experience spans investment banking, corporate secretarial advisory, capital markets transactions, listed company compliance, regulatory matters etc. This experience forms the foundation of the practical guidance and execution support provided by our firm to its clients.

Additional professional credentials and career highlights are available on the LinkedIn profile of our founder.
